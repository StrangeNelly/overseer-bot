# Groupie (working title)

A web dashboard for crypto Telegram groups. It connects to your group, tracks the tokens and pre-launch projects members post ("calls"), and gives everyone a simple board to check — so nothing gets missed in a busy chat.

## Core ideas

- **Read-only board, not another feed.** Nobody posts to the app; it summarises what the group already curated.
- **Time-filtered call board.** See which tokens were called in the last 6/12/24 hours, 3 days, week, or month, with live market data and links out to trading apps (Axiom etc.).
- **Launch monitoring.** Post `/groupie addlaunchmonitor @project` in Telegram and the app watches that X account for contract addresses and launch announcements.
- **Died-after-call section.** Rugged/dead calls surface separately; the group decides whether to keep or purge them.
- **v1 chain:** Robinhood Chain (#HOOD). Multi-chain and multi-group SaaS later.
